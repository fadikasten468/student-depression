Conclusion

What did I find?

Academic Pressure: Students with higher academic pressure (4-5) have significantly higher depression rates.

Sleep Duration: Students who sleep less than 5 hours have the highest depression rates. Students sleeping 7-8 hours have the lowest.

Financial Stress: Higher financial stress is clearly linked to higher depression rates.
